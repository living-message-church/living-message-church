# Homepage art direction review

Review date: 2026-08-06  
Scope: emotional storytelling and focal hierarchy; the established design system, routes, and content models remain unchanged

## Creative standard

Each homepage section must have one clear focal point, one emotional job, and one reason to be remembered. Photography should carry human feeling whenever approved imagery exists. Interface elements remain subordinate to the story.

## Launch discipline

- Empty operational states should not interrupt the homepage narrative.
- No decorative motion, badges, or overlays are added to manufacture emotion.
- Interior pages remain task-oriented: their photography establishes atmosphere, while factual sections prioritize clarity over campaign-style moments.
- Staged and no-index routes are not given invented emotional claims while their content remains verification-gated.
